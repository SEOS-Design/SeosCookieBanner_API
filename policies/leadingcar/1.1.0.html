1. Introduction

This cookie policy explains how cookies are used on this website, what they are used for and how you can manage your settings.

Cookies may be placed either by the website operator or by third-party services integrated into the website.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet or mobile) when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to improve the user experience and to provide information to the website operator.

3. Legal basis for the use of cookies

The use of strictly necessary cookies is based on the website operator's legitimate interest in ensuring the correct functioning of the website.

All other cookies are used only after you have given your explicit consent.

4. Managing your consent

When you visit the website for the first time, you are asked to make a choice regarding the use of cookies. Your settings are saved to ensure that your choices are respected on future visits.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by opening the cookie settings through the link or button available on the website.

5. Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly and cannot be disabled. They do not require your consent.

Name Purpose Duration
consent_status Stores your overall cookie consent choice. 30 days
consent_choices Stores detailed settings for the cookie categories. 30 days
client_consent_id A unique ID linked to your browser, used to demonstrate that valid consent has been given. It contains no information that directly identifies you. 365 days

With your consent, this website may use third-party services such as analytics or marketing tools. These cookies are used to understand how visitors interact with the website and to improve its functionality and content.

This website uses Google Consent Mode to ensure that Google services respect your consent choices. Depending on your choice, Google tags adjust their behaviour accordingly.
